Subject: Question about research positions in the [lab name] lab

Dear Professor [last name],

I'm a sophomore studying [subfield] at [university]. I attended your talk on [talk title] at [event name] last week and found your approach to [research topic] really interesting, especially the part about [specific detail from talk].

I'm reaching out because I'd love to learn more about the research your lab is doing and whether there might be any openings for an undergraduate research assistant this semester. I've attached my resume, which includes coursework in [relevant courses] and a project I did on [related project].

Would you have 15 minutes in the next couple of weeks to talk about your lab's current work? I'm free most weekday afternoons and happy to work around your schedule.

Thank you for your time,
[student name]
