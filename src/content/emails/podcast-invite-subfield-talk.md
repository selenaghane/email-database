annotations:
  - quote: "we're a student-run podcast on [subfield]"
    note: States what the podcast is in one clause, before the ask, without overselling its size.
  - quote: "your panel answer about [panel topic]"
    note: References a specific moment from a specific event rather than the recipient's work in general.
  - quote: "a 30-minute recorded conversation"
    note: Names the format and time commitment up front instead of leaving it open-ended.
  - quote: "we can work around your schedule and record any weekday"
    note: Pre-answers the scheduling question so accepting doesn't require negotiating logistics first.
  - quote: "Subject: Podcast invite"
    note: Subject states exactly what the email is before it's opened.

Subject: Podcast invite: 30 min on [subfield] for [podcast name]

Hi [first name],

My name is [student name] and I host [podcast name] — we're a student-run podcast on [subfield] aimed at undergrads considering the field. I saw you on the [panel topic] panel at [event name] and really appreciated your panel answer about [panel topic]; it's the kind of thing our listeners rarely get to hear explained that plainly.

I'd love to have you on for a 30-minute recorded conversation about [topic], with a few questions sent in advance if that's helpful. We publish on [platform] and typically get [rough listener count] listens per episode.

If you're open to it, we can work around your schedule and record any weekday over the next month. No worries at all if now isn't a good time.

Thanks for considering it,
[student name]
